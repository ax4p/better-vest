Picking the folder
  1. Click Update. The first time, Chrome asks for a folder.
  2. Pick your Better Vest folder, the one you loaded on chrome://extensions, or any folder it is in, like Documents.
  3. Allow it to edit files.

Not sure where it is? On chrome://extensions, click Details on Better Vest: Source shows the folder. On a Mac, folders whose name starts with a dot are hidden in the picker: press Cmd Shift . to show them.
