Better Vest by Astral - how to install (2 minutes, Chrome / Brave / Edge / Arc)

1. Download better-vest-8.0.5.zip and double-click it.
   You now have a folder called "better-vest-8.0.5". Keep it somewhere safe
   (Documents is fine). Chrome loads the extension from this folder, so don't delete it.

2. Open Chrome and type in the address bar:  chrome://extensions
   (Brave: brave://extensions - Edge: edge://extensions)

3. Top-right corner: turn ON "Developer mode".

4. Click "Load unpacked" (top-left) and pick the "better-vest-8.0.5" folder.
   "Better Vest by Astral" appears in the list. Done.

5. Pin it: click the puzzle icon in Chrome's toolbar and pin Better Vest.

6. Open https://next.vestmarkets.com - the ASTRAL dock appears at the top.

7. Calendar: click "Calendar" in the dock (or press Alt+J), then "Build my calendar".
   It reads your own trade history from Vest (read-only) and keeps it on this computer.


WHAT YOU GET
- Exec strip: size presets per account (5K / 10K / 25K / custom), LONG / SHORT / FLAT with a stop and target in points.
- Chart TP/SL: your position, TP and SL on the chart. Drag the TP / SL buttons or labels to set them with the dollar value live.
  The BE button moves the stop to breakeven (entry + a small share of the open profit; every part is a setting).
- Macros (hotkeys): W = long, S = short, E = limit buy at the best bid, Q = limit sell at the best ask, H = breakeven.
  They use Vest's own ticket, ignore the keys while you type, and stop TradingView's type-to-search from opening.
  The dock button "Macros" is the master switch; Settings > Hotkeys remaps every key.
  Order keys (W S E Q) work on NQ and MNQ with the Exec strip showing; E and Q also need Chart TP/SL on, to see your position.
  Standard installs start with Macros OFF (and E / Q unarmed): switch them on in the dock / Settings when you want them.
- Copy trader: the Copy button in the dock. Pick a leader account and the accounts that follow it, and every order,
  stop, target, reduce, reverse and flatten on the leader is repeated on them at each one's ratio. It starts off, asks
  you to read a short risk note first and never switches itself on after a reload. Alt+Shift+K stops it.
- Focus mode, themes (Dark is the default), Calendar (PnL per day, risk, RR, payouts; Alt+J).

Using the Tampermonkey version, or the other edition of this extension, too? Keep only ONE on:
turn the others OFF, otherwise the dock and the overlay show twice (the suite also refuses to start a second copy). Your settings carry over automatically.

UPDATING: from 7.4 on it updates itself. When a new version is out, the dock shows an
"Update" button (and the toolbar icon says NEW). Click it, then Update. The first time,
Chrome asks for this folder: pick it and allow editing. Every file is checked against
a signed list before anything is written. Leave Developer mode on.
By hand (or coming from 7.3): unzip the new zip over the old folder (replace files),
then on chrome://extensions press the circular arrow on the Better Vest card.

Chrome may show "Disable developer mode extensions" on start-up. Click the X /
"Keep" - it's a normal warning for extensions not installed from the Chrome Web Store.
